### 2. 👮‍♂️ Sudden Bengaluru Traffic Police (BTP) License Check (`traffic_police.glb`)
- **Roadside BTP Nakabandi & Barricade**: BTP officers wearing the signature **white pith helmet (topi)**, **lime-green high-visibility reflective vest** over khaki uniform, aviator sunglasses, and holding a **glowing red LED traffic baton** wait beside a bilingual `ಬೆಂಗಳೂರು ಸಂಚಾರ ಪೊಲೀಸ್ / BENGALURU TRAFFIC POLICE` steel barricade with flashing red-and-blue strobe beacons.
- **Sudden Whistle & Step-Out**: As your Auto-Rickshaw approaches within range, the cop blows a shrill **pea-whistle (`"PRRREEEET!"`)**, steps into your lane, waves his red LED baton, and shouts:
  > **👮‍♂️ *"ಏಯ್ ಆಟೋ! ಸೈಡ್ ಹಾಕಿ! DL & RC ತೋರಿಸಿ!"* ("Ey Auto! Pull over! Show your License!")**
- **3 Ways to Handle the BTP Cop**:
  1. **Flash DigiLocker License (`L` key or `🪪 Show DL [L]` HUD button)**: Instantly verifies your DL, RC & Green Emission certificate (`"ಸರಿ ಬಿಡಿ! ಮುಂದೆ ಹೋಗಿ!"`), waves the officer back to the kerb, and awards a **`+₹100` Law-Abiding Auto Bonus**!
  2. **Swerve & Dodge**: Change lanes cleanly around the checkpost (`+₹60` Evasion Bonus while the cop threatens a camera challan).
  3. **Busted (`e-Challan` Fine)**: Colliding with the officer triggers a police siren burst, brakes your auto hard, and deducts a **`-₹100` e-Challan fine** from your meter!

### 3. 🐕 Occasional Darting Bengaluru Stray Dog (`stray_dog.glb`)
- **Authentic Indian Pariah Dog (Indie Doggo)**: Procedurally modeled in Blender with a warm terracotta/tawny coat, white paws, alert perked ears, a collar, and the classic **upward-curled Indie tail**.
- **Sudden Road Darting & 4-Leg Gallop Cycle**: Stray dogs wait near roadside stalls and suddenly bolt across the 6-lane highway with a synthesized double-bark (**`"ಬೌ-ಬೌ! BOW-WOW!"`**) and articulated 4-leg bounding animation.
- **Interactive Reactions**:
  - **Blast Auto Horn (`H`)**: The stray dog barks back and sprints **2x faster** to clear your lane!
  - **Gravity Glitch Hop (`SPACE`)**: Jump cleanly over a darting dog to earn a **`+₹75` Good Boy Hop Bonus**!

---

### 4. 🎨 Pro-Game PBR Visuals, PMREM Lighting & Post-Processing
- **Three.js `RoomEnvironment` PMREM Reflections**: Realistic metallic sheen on vehicle bodywork, chrome handlebars, and glass windshields.
- **ACES Filmic Tone Mapping + `UnrealBloomPass`**: Calibrated exposure (`0.95`) and selective bloom (`threshold: 0.94`) so LED destination boards, taillights, red/blue police strobes, and illuminated hoardings glow naturally while keeping vehicle paint rich and saturated.
- **Multi-Grain Asphalt & Golden-Hour Sky Dome**: Procedural 6-lane highway texture with micro-aggregate grain and glossy tire wear tracks beneath a dusk/golden-hour Bengaluru horizon.

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Option 1: Open Directly (Zero-Install Single-File Bundle)
Because [`index.html`](index.html) embeds all **12 Blender `.glb` models** as gzip-compressed base64 fallbacks (`DecompressionStream('gzip')`), you can open `index.html` directly in any modern browser.

### Option 2: Run Local HTTP Server
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8765
```
Then open **`http://localhost:8765/index.html`** in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

#### Optional URL Query Parameters
- `http://localhost:8765/index.html?autostart=low` — Launch directly into **Low (`ಕಬ್ಬನ್ ಪಾರ್ಕ್ ಬೆಳಿಗ್ಗೆ | Cubbon Park Morning`)** mode.
- `http://localhost:8765/index.html?autostart=medium` — Launch directly into **Medium (`ಇಂದಿರಾನಗರ ಸಂಜೆ ರಶ್ | Indiranagar 6 PM Rush`)** mode.
- `http://localhost:8765/index.html?autostart=medium&copcheck=1&hop=1` — Launch with an active **BTP Traffic Police license check** and **Gravity Hop**.
- `http://localhost:8765/index.html?autostart=complex&rain=1&snapwheel=1&copcheck=1` — Launch directly into **Peak Hour (`ಸಿಲ್ಕ್ ಬೋರ್ಡ್ ಟ್ರಾಫಿಕ್ | Silk Board 8 PM Gridlock`)** with monsoon downpour, hydroplaning, and BTP nakabandi.

## 🛠️ Rebuilding 3D Assets & HTML Bundle

1. **Regenerate all 12 PBR `.glb` models & studio showcase render in Blender**:
   ```bash
   /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --python build_assets_blender.py
   ```
   *(Or run `python3 build_assets_blender.py` to execute via a running Blender MCP socket on `127.0.0.1:9876` — see [`agents.md`](agents.md) for details.)*

2. **Compile `index.html` from `build_game_html.py` and `assets/*.glb`**:
   ```bash
   python3 build_game_html.py
   ```
